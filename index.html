Overview

Modern living, built for the way you actually live.

Completed in 2020 and designed by award-winning architect Ben Waechter, Wygant Flats offers eight spacious one- and two-bedroom homes averaging over 1,100 square feet. Every residence was originally built to condo standards — real wood floors, quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, and gas fireplaces — with an open-concept layout and floor-to-ceiling windows that fill each home with natural light.

Location

Portland's Overlook neighborhood

One block off N Interstate Ave and N Alberta St, Wygant Flats sits in one of Portland's most walkable and connected neighborhoods — close to transit, parks, and the city's best food and coffee.

01

Transit & Commute

Just one block from N Interstate Ave and the MAX Light Rail Yellow Line, with I-5 access two blocks away. A true urban commuter's location.

02

Parks & Outdoors

Nine parks within a mile, including Overlook Park, Mocks Crest, and Sumner-Albina City Park — plus expansive views of the Willamette River and city skyline.

03

Food & Shopping

Minutes from New Seasons Market and the trendy Mississippi Ave, N Williams, and Alberta Arts District corridors, packed with restaurants, cafes, and shops.
